# Spice and Wolf, Volume 7

> "It was the virtue of all merchants that even if they had been prepared to kill each other yesterday, if their interests were aligned today, they'd happily shake hands."

## Synopsis

Using Holo's wolf form, Lawrence, Holo and Col manage to intercept Eve before she arrives in Kerube, much to Eve's shock. The four of them enter Kerube together. 

The next day, Holo is extremely sore from so much running, so when Even comes to fetch them to talk, they leave Holo behind to rest. Lawrence shares what he's learned so far with Eve, and the two chat at length. Despite Holo's hostile disposition and how Eve tricked them previously, the two get continue to get along well, and like Holo, Eve takes a liking to Col. Eve notes that the company repeatedly sending copper coin orders is called the Debau Company, based in a mining town called Lesko. Though most people only know it as rumor, she confirms that the Debau Company and Jean Company are indeed working together to search for the wolf bones. Though she likens the task to a wild goose chase, she opts to humor Lawrence as a show of goodwill, writing him a letter of introduction for the Jean Company. Before Lawrence and Col leave, Eve also mentions that she's planning to depart south the next day, and invites them to come have dinner with her if they have time.
